Customer Keys (balance in USD)

Make a key with a fixed balance (for example $1, $2, $5). The customer pastes it in the site's Auto tab. Every order is charged from that key at the customer rates you set in Service Mapping below, and the key switches itself off when its balance is used up. When they want more, take payment and make a new key (or top the same key up).
